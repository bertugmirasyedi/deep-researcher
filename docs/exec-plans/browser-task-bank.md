# Browser Task Bank and Bootstrap Data Mix

- **Issue**: BER-171
- **Status**: Draft task-bank plan
- **Decision source**: [D018](../decisions.md#d018-browser-native-single-agent-curriculum-over-plain-teacher-distillation) and [BER-163 browser-native curriculum](browser-native-curriculum.md)
- **Last updated**: 2026-06-15

## Scope and contamination policy

This bank defines benchmark-informed **patterns and freshly authored templates**, not copied benchmark items. Actual public benchmark examples from BrowseComp, GAIA, WebWalkerQA, DeepResearch Bench/ResearchRubrics, WebArena, WebVoyager, Mind2Web, or similar browser-agent suites should stay `eval_only` unless the item is explicitly licensed and intentionally allocated to training after dedupe review. Training tasks should be generated from the templates below with fresh entities, dates, domains, and answer keys produced by a browser-only teacher trace plus deterministic verifier gates.

Web search was unavailable in this worker environment, so the scouting below uses known benchmark families and direct family knowledge rather than newly fetched benchmark pages. The families are used as **shape constraints**: exact-answer long-tail web research (BrowseComp), multi-step assistant web/reasoning (GAIA), website traversal QA (WebWalkerQA), long-form research synthesis and rubric-style evaluation (DeepResearch/ResearchRubrics), and browser navigation/action patterns (WebArena, WebVoyager, Mind2Web).

## Benchmark family map

| Family | Use in this bank | Split guidance | Main contamination concern |
|---|---|---|---|
| BrowseComp-style browser research | Exact-answer, long-tail, obscure-fact tasks requiring search reformulation and source confirmation | Template-derived train/val; actual benchmark items `eval_only` | High if prompts/entities are copied |
| GAIA-like web/reasoning | Multi-step assistant questions combining retrieval, arithmetic, comparison, and constraints | Freshly authored train/val; benchmark items `eval_only` | Medium/high due public questions |
| WebWalkerQA / web navigation QA | Site traversal, following menus/links, finding answers buried in pages | Fresh site/entity instantiations; benchmark pages `eval_only` | Medium if exact website-question pair copied |
| DeepResearch / ResearchRubrics-style | Multi-source cited synthesis, uncertainty, source quality, answer structure | Eval inspiration and verifier design; training only with fresh prompts | High for public eval prompts |
| WebArena / WebVoyager / Mind2Web | Browser-action patterns: forms, menus, filters, comparison pages, navigation recovery | Use as navigation behavior templates, not task text | Medium; actions can overfit to benchmark sites |
| Negative/reward-hacking fixtures | Synthetic bad traces and contrastive prompts for verifier hardening | Train auxiliary / negative / eval regression | Low when generated synthetically |

## Bucket definitions

The bank covers every BER-163 bucket: `browser_basics`, `search_navigation_retry`, `single_page_evidence`, `multi_source_evidence_cards`, `context_compression_recall`, `gap_assessment_uncertainty`, `short_cited_synthesis`, `hard_browser_research`, and `negative_contrastive`.

Label conventions:

- `SFT-A`: browser actions plus visible research/context primitives.
- `SFT-B aux`: same policy trace with bounded observation-prediction targets such as page type, relevance, next-observation delta, or bad-action risk.
- `negative`: contrastive/reward-hacking fixture; not used as positive policy imitation unless rendering explicitly marks the rejected behavior.
- `eval`: validation/evaluation candidate; no training if copied from benchmark, public eval, or answer-key leak risk.

## Initial staged mixture recommendation for first 50-100 trace bootstrap

For the first bootstrap, prioritize verifier-debuggable traces over ambitious long-horizon research. Generate with browser-only teacher traces, reject traces with forbidden tools, missing citations, fake quotes, inaccessible pages, or empty context primitives.

### Suggested 80-trace target mix

| Bucket group | Share | Approx. traces in 80 | Rationale |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Browser basics + single-page evidence | 20% | 16 | Build reliable search/goto/click/extract/final-answer mechanics. |
| Search/navigation/retry | 15% | 12 | Teach ambiguity handling, result triage, and recovery from bad first hits. |
| Multi-source evidence cards + short synthesis | 25% | 20 | Main positive research behavior; enough source diversity for citation gates. |
| Context compression/recall | 10% | 8 | Introduce visible memory only where useful, avoiding primitive spam. |
| Gap assessment/uncertainty/absence | 15% | 12 | Teach abstention, conflict handling, and checked-places reporting early. |
| Hard BrowseComp-style browser research | 5% | 4 | Keep hard exact-answer search present but not dominant before basics work. |
| Negative/contrastive/reward-hacking fixtures | 10% | 8 | Train verifier boundaries: fake citations, source stuffing, blocked pages, forbidden tools. |

If only 50 traces are affordable, preserve proportions with a minimum floor of: 8 basics, 7 retry, 12 multi-source/synthesis, 5 context, 8 gap, 3 hard BrowseComp-style, 7 negative.

### SFT-A vs SFT-B auxiliary proportions

- Render **100%** of positive traces for SFT-A policy/process targets.
- Add SFT-B bounded auxiliary targets to **35-45%** of positive traces, concentrated on navigation/retry, context, gap, and hard research tasks where page relevance, bad-action risk, and observation deltas matter.
- Include negative/contrastive fixtures as **10-15%** of total rendered examples. Of those, about half should be SFT-A repair/rewrite targets and half SFT-B auxiliary/bad-action classification targets.
- Keep actual eval-only benchmark-derived items out of both SFT-A and SFT-B training renders.

### Replay and sampling-with-replacement policy

- Train with weighted sampling with replacement, as D018 recommends, but cap repeats per concrete task instance at **3 per epoch-equivalent** and log repeat counts plus effective sample size.
- Use temperature-smoothed bucket weights so small critical buckets (gap, negative, context) remain visible without over-repeating the same instances.
- Keep easy browser/navigation/evidence examples in replay during later stages; do not replace them completely with hard research tasks.
- Deduplicate by normalized prompt, answer key, target entity, visited canonical domains, and final answer string.

### Train/val/eval split policy

- Split by **template instantiation and target entity**, not by trace rendering. No entity or exact answer key should appear across train and val/eval.
- Initial generated bank: **70% train / 15% validation / 15% internal eval**, with validation/eval sampled without replacement.
- Public benchmark items, copied benchmark prompts, or prompts likely seen in benchmark leaderboards: `eval_only` and isolated from training.
- Negative fixtures can have a separate regression split: keep at least 20% of fixture types held out to test verifier generalization to unseen hacks.

## Raw-student difficulty probe sample

This worker **does not run** the student or browser/model runtime. Per dispatch scope, the deliverable is a concrete capped sample suitable for a later raw-student diagnostic run. The acceptance-result fields that require actual completion rate, verifier pass rate, keepable count, and failure modes remain blocked until a coordinator explicitly dispatches a runtime worker with browser harness credentials and student endpoint access.

Probe protocol:
