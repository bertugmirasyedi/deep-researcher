01

Where this sits

The eval harness (BER-151), the single-agent organism (BER-140), and the floor check (BER-139) are done. D013 landed a clean outcome 1 — the raw student is reliable on the harness (mean 0.47, 5/5, quality varied 0.21–0.85) once the web-tool output bug was fixed. We now turn on evolution. The student stays fixed so the evolved scaffold is tuned for the exact model that later gets SFT (BER-145) and RL (BER-146); a clean ablation chain follows.

Fixed Integrity boundary

  • Task policy — the student model; non-evolvable (already wired as the deep-researcher-student alias).
  • Evaluator + judge — evaluate(), the BER-150 Bedrock Opus 4.8 rubric judge, and the prompt set.
  • Evolution loop — evolve.ts, NSGA-II ranking, archive, promotion gate.
  • Meta-agent model — frontier mutation proposer; never sees per-criterion rubric detail (hidden-mode redaction).

Evolvable The organism

  • SYSTEM.md — research strategy, source-tiering, citation rules, report structure.
  • Toolset wiring — when/how to call web_search / fetch_content; the 30 KB content cap is fixed, the usage policy is not.
  • Thresholds — sub-question count, search depth, refinement triggers (capabilities, not mandates).
  • Model config — student max_tokens, thinking level, per-stage budgets.
02

Objectives & promotion

Multi-objective: don't collapse quality, cost, and latency into one number — keep the Pareto front so we can pick a point afterward. The held-out set is the guardrail against the meta-agent overfitting the five evolution prompts.

Objective
Dir
Measure
Quality
MAX
Composite weighted-rubric score (0–1) from the BER-150 judge — same one the floor check used.
Cost
MIN
Total tokens per run. Student is free on vLLM, but tokens still proxy latency/context pressure — keep it honest.
Latency
MIN
Wall-clock per run. The 30 KB cap already tamed the 600 s stalls; this keeps organisms from drifting back to bloat.

Promotion gate: an archive organism is promoted only if its held-out composite is within 5% of its evolution-set composite (no overfit) and ≥ the gen-0 baseline held-out composite (no regression). Held-out scores are aggregate-only and never fed back to the meta-agent.

03

Slices

Four reviewable slices on bm/feat/deep-researcher-evolution. Each ends at a checkpoint; the run only proceeds past slice 3 if the gate in slice 2 is green.

Slice 1

Turn on the meta-agent loop

Today the deep-researcher kind runs eval-only. Wire evolve.ts + the meta-agent for it: seed the archive with the gen-0 organism, confirm a single mutate→eval→rank→archive cycle completes, and that the meta-agent's feedback excludes per-criterion rubric detail (integrity check).

Slice 2 · gate

Noise control & budget guardrails

Within-prompt reward variance is real (BER-150: sd ≈ 0.07–0.10). Use hyperagent's racing to repeat-eval each organism until fitness separates, so the meta-agent optimizes signal not draw-luck. Set per-run token + timeout caps and a total judge-call budget cap (re-cost in BER-141). Gate: group advantages are non-degenerate and a re-eval of gen-0 reproduces ~0.47 ± noise.

src/racingstageGateexit: stable gen-0 fitness
Slice 3

Run the evolution

Small population (≈10/gen), a handful of generations, on the evolution prompts. After each generation, validate the top-Pareto organisms on the held-out set (logged, not shown to the meta-agent). Stop on budget cap or when generation-over-generation held-out gain < noise.

Slice 4

Freeze, ablate, write up

Promote the winner through the gate, freeze + archive it, and record the one-number-per-stage ablation: gen-0 → evolved harness (the +SFT / +RL stages come later). Note any crutches the student-tuned scaffold baked in — input for the P2O re-evolution after RL (BER-147).

The dominant cost is judge calls, not the student: ≈ prompts × population × generations × racing-repeats. At 5 × 10 × 5 × 4 that is ~1,000 judge calls — must be priced and capped under BER-141 before slice 3 launches.
