## Executive Summary

Europa Universalis V was released November 4, 2025 by Paradox Tinto (Barcelona), directed by Johan Andersson, after development since 2020 under the codename "Project Caesar" [S1][S2]. As of the research date, the live build is **Patch 1.1.10** (checksum b0ac, released March 17, 2026), part of the 1.1 "Rossbach" update line [S2][S3]. The major **1.2 "Echinades"** update and first DLC **"Fate of the Phoenix"** were scheduled for release on May 6, 2026 — one day after this research was conducted [S2][S4].

Community meta for beginner nation picks is **not unanimous**: Power Up Gaming ranks Castile #1 overall ("best beginner-friendly nation") [S5], while the community wiki recommends Portugal first for its safety [S6]. France, often assumed to be the top pick, is ranked #3 by Power Up Gaming and placed last in the community wiki's progression route, with the caveat that "early continental wars can be overwhelming to beginners" [S5][S6].

The upcoming 1.2 update introduces a significant military overhaul (splitting units into light/heavy infantry and cavalry variants), a logistics rework (10× food consumption, reduced supply range), city walls, naval combat changes, and a new "Movements" system for cultural/religious spread [S7].

## Findings

### SQ1: Game Status & Patch Context

**Official.** EU5 released November 4, 2025 by Paradox Tinto, directed by Johan Andersson. Developed since 2020 as "Project Caesar" [S1][S2].

**Official.** The live build as of May 5, 2026 is **Patch 1.1.10** (checksum b0ac, released March 17, 2026). The patch history runs: 1.0.X series (launch through 1.0.11, January 2026) → 1.1 "Rossbach" line (1.1.9 → 1.1.10) [S2][S3].

**Official.** The **1.2 "Echinades"** update and **"Fate of the Phoenix"** DLC were scheduled for release on May 6, 2026 [S2][S4].

Confidence: **high** — all patch dates, version numbers, and release dates are directly from official Paradox sources and Wikipedia.

### SQ2: Current Meta & Beginner Starts

⚠️ *This section is community consensus, not official design intent.*

**Community opinion.** Sources disagree on a single best beginner nation:

- **Castile** — ranked #1 by Power Up Gaming: "best beginner-friendly nation with massive conquest and colonization potential" [S5].
- **Portugal** — ranked #1 by community wiki: "safest start for learning trade," isolated from major land threats [S6].
- **France** — ranked #3 by Power Up Gaming; community wiki places it last in their recommended progression. Strong economy but "early continental wars can be overwhelming to beginners" [S5][S6].
- **Ottomans** — strong military and mission trees, but slower start in the 1337 bookmark [S5].

**Community opinion.** Recommended progression route (community wiki): Portugal → England → Castile → Ottomans → France, each teaching progressively harder skills [S6].

**Official.** Key live mechanics (1.1 Rossbach) noted by press coverage: complacency mechanic (empires with no threats suffer penalties), food decay (excess food decays; stockpiles boost pop growth) [S4].

**Community opinion.** Community wiki advises: build marketplaces before trade routes (zero-capacity markets block all trade), avoid two-front wars early, don't overbuild (unstaffed buildings waste resources) [S6].

Confidence: **high** for official mechanics; **medium** for meta opinions (community guides from Nov 2025–Mar 2026, pre-1.2 release).

### SQ3: Upcoming Changes in 1.2 Echinades (Releasing May 6, 2026)

**Official.** All mechanics below are from Tinto Talks #104 (April 15, 2026) [S7]:

- **4-way unit split**: light infantry / heavy infantry / light cavalry / heavy cavalry — creates national identity (Swedish musketeers play differently from Crimean horse archers). Light units are faster but fragile; heavy units are the grinding core.
- **Logistics rework**: armies consume roughly 10× more food; base logistics range reduced from 50 to 30; generals' logistics bonus halved.
- **City walls**: unique building granting minimum fort level 1.
- **Naval combat**: minimum 3-day battles; scattered retreats; improved AI retreat logic.
- **Movements system**: grassroots cultural/religious spread via trade routes, armies, and adjacency.
- **Mercenaries**: made roughly 20–25% more expensive.
- **Combined arms**: auxiliaries/transport vessels no longer dilute the combined arms bonus.

Confidence: **high** — all claims sourced directly from official developer diary.
